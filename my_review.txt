Review of n.txt

SECTION 1: Main Problems

1. Review methodology is missing: no databases, keywords, date range, or paper-selection criteria.

2. Undefined reference: \ref{sec:challenges} is used, but the section label does not exist.

3. Typo in label: sec:methology should be sec:methodology.

4. Required files are missing: Figures/TaxonomyMap.png and sn-bibliography.bib.

5. The taxonomy mixes stages, mechanisms, learning objectives, and model families. These categories are not fully consistent.

6. Adaptive fusion is listed as a stage, although it can be used at any fusion stage.

7. Asynchronous data is mentioned but not discussed as deeply as missing, noisy, and imbalanced data.

8. Tables mostly organize papers; they do not provide enough comparable results or experimental conditions.

9. Duplicate declarations/template text remains at the end.

10. Some grammar, terminology, author-name, citation, and consistency issues remain.


SECTION 2: Main Improvements

1. Add a short, reproducible review-methodology section.

2. Apply the seven taxonomy dimensions to a consistent table of representative papers.

3. Separate fusion stage, mechanism, learning objective, architecture, and data condition more clearly.

4. Treat adaptive fusion as a cross-cutting property, not a separate stage.

5. Add a dedicated discussion of asynchronous fusion and temporal alignment.

6. Improve benchmark comparison with dataset version, split, task, metric, and limitations.

7. Add unimodal baselines, modality ablations, robustness tests, efficiency, and calibration comparisons.

8. Remove repeated explanations and unsupported broad claims.

9. Fix the undefined reference, label typo, missing files, duplicate declarations, and author-name inconsistency.

10. Rename n.txt to a .tex file and compile-test the manuscript before submission.
